    Check the total cost and warehouse photos

    After finding a listing, record the seller’s current price, size and color. If you plan to order with iTaoBuy, confirm that it accepts the source link before paying.

    Estimate the landed cost

    Add the item, shipping and fees in CNY. Convert destination charges into CNY first. For a shared parcel, enter the portion of shipping you expect this item to use.

    Enter an item price to estimate the total.

    Example in CNY: ¥105 for the item + ¥12 domestic delivery + ¥8 in fees + ¥95 international shipping + ¥25 destination charges = ¥245. Ask how the carrier calculates billable weight when comparing bulky items.

    Review warehouse photos by category

    • Shoes: Ask for both size labels, both soles and a side-by-side heel photo. Check that the labels, shape and color match your order.
    • Hoodies and tees: Ask for chest width and body length measured flat. Compare them with a garment you own and check print placement and seams.
    • Jackets: Ask for sleeve and chest measurements plus close-ups of the zipper, cuffs and lining.

    If a photo leaves a detail unclear, ask for that specific angle or measurement before approving shipment. Photos show visible details. Fabric performance and brand authenticity require separate verification.

    iTaoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

    What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

    It is a catalog-style guide shoppers use to discover and compare items before ordering through a shopping agent. This independent guide uses selected HipobuyFind listings as a starting point.

    Where do the product links lead?

    Each product name opens its own HipobuyFind listing. Use that listing to find the seller link, then check whether your chosen agent supports it.

    Do the listed prices include shipping?

    The catalog shows item prices in CNY. Estimate domestic delivery, agent or payment fees, international shipping, and any destination taxes or duties separately.

    What should I do if a listing changes?

    Open the current product and seller pages. Check the title, photos, price and available variants against your shortlist. If the seller replaced the item, verify the new listing from scratch before ordering.
